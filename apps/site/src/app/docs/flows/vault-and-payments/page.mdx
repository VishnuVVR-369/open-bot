# Vault and payments

Teammates need to sign in to sites and sometimes pay for things.
The vault lets them use your logins and cards without ever reading them, and budgets decide how much they may spend.

## The vault

A vault item (`vault_items`) is a **login** or a **card**:

| Kind  | Fields                                   | Also stored                               |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| login | `username`, `password`                   | The site it belongs to, like `github.com` |
| card  | `number`, `expiry`, `cvc`, `name`, `zip` | A hint, like "Visa •• 4242"               |

The fields are stored as one JSON object, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using `ENCRYPTION_KEY`.
Each item lists the teammates allowed to use it.

`vault_list` shows a teammate the items it may use, by name, kind, site and hint, and its spending budget.
It never returns a secret.

## Filling a field

The teammate clicks the field on its screen, then calls `vault_fill` with an item and a field name.
It never sees the value.

The checks, in order:

1. **Access.** The item must exist and allow this teammate.
2. **Field.** The field must belong to the item's kind.
3. **Purchase (cards).** A card fills only while an approved purchase for that card is open. An approval opens it for 30 minutes (`PURCHASE_WINDOW_MS`).
4. **Lease.** botd takes the screen's lease for the run, as for any desktop action. If you hold the screen, the fill fails.
5. **Site (logins).** botd lists every visible page over the DevTools pipe. If any of them isn't on the login's site or one of its subdomains, it refuses: "The browser is showing evil.example, not github.com, so it wasn't filled." A misleading page can't collect a password this way.
6. **Typing.** botd types the value into whatever has keyboard focus with `xdotool type --file -`, reading the secret from **stdin**, not from the arguments, because any process on the computer can list another process's arguments.

The item's `last_used_at` is updated, and the timeline shows "Filled the GitHub password", never the value.

<Callout kind="warning">
  A page can still show what was typed. A card number on a checkout page, for example, can appear in
  the next screenshot the teammate takes. The vault keeps secrets out of tool results, logs and
  chats, not off the screen.
</Callout>

## Budgets

Each teammate can have a monthly budget (`bots.monthly_budget_cents`) and an auto-approve amount (`bots.auto_approve_cents`).
Without a budget, it can't pay for anything.

Spending is the sum of the teammate's purchases this month (from the first of the month, UTC) that weren't denied or called off.

## A purchase

The developer instructions tell every teammate to pay only with a vault card, and only through this flow:

1. **`start_purchase`** with the exact total, the shop, what it is, and the card.
2. `budgetDecision()` checks it:
   - over what's left of the budget: refused, and the teammate is told not to buy it;
   - at or under the auto-approve amount: approved at once;
   - otherwise: a `purchase` approval goes to you.
3. Once approved, the card fills for 30 minutes. The teammate fills it with `vault_fill` and places the order.
4. **`finish_purchase`** records the order number, or `paid: false` if it didn't go through. A purchase that didn't go through stops counting against the budget.

A purchase approval can't be covered by an Always allow rule.
The only way to let small payments through without asking is the auto-approve amount.

If you approve after the teammate stopped waiting, a new run tells it the purchase was approved, that the card fills for the next 30 minutes, and to call `finish_purchase` when it's done.

Each teammate's page shows its spending this month, with every purchase and its status.
